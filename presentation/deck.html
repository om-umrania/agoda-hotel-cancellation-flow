02 · CONTEXT
Why this moment matters

The most anxious step in travel, and our best chance to earn trust.

😣 The problem

Users can book in Voya but can't cancel in Voya. When plans change they call support or dispute with their bank, cost, chargebacks, churn, and a lost trust moment.

🎯 The goal

Let users cancel themselves, seeing exactly what they'll be refunded and when, before they commit. The refund shown is the refund paid.

★ North star

"No surprises." Transparency over optimism, we trade a few completed cancellations for far fewer disputes and far more repeat bookings.

How we'll know it worked
↗ Self-serve rate
Share of cancellations done in-app without support.
↘ Tickets & disputes
Cancellation tickets and refund chargebacks fall.
◎ Preview drop-off
Where trust breaks, the screen that matters most.
↺ Rebooking rate
Revenue recovered right after a cancellation.
03 · GROUND RULES
Assumptions & scope

What we're designing, and what we're deliberately not.

1
Partner Fulfillment. Voya owns payment + post-booking (incl. cancellation) via Agoda.
2
Full cancellation of one confirmed, prepaid booking made in-app.
3
Booking linkage. Each booking stores Agoda's bookingId mapped to the user.
4
Server-authoritative refund. Amount comes from Agoda's Preview call, never computed on device.
5
Refund to origin. Settled by Agoda/rails; Voya surfaces status only.
6
Re-auth (biometric) gates the destructive confirm step.
7
Locale. Booking currency; deadlines in hotel local time.
8
Window. Cancel up to 1 day before check-in, per Agoda; else assisted.
9
Idempotent confirm. Agoda reference + our key, never double-cancels.
10
~120 s SLA tolerated via a processing state & async PENDING.

Out of scope · v1

  • Date / room modifications
  • Partial (per-room / per-night) cancel
  • No-show handling
  • Group / corporate bookings
  • Agent / offline cancellation

Captured as future scope in the PRD.

09 · WIREFRAME ③   CONFIRM + RE-AUTH
Wireframe ③, the destructive step

Deliberate confirmation, with re-authentication.

04 · Confirm + Face ID → Processing (~120s)

🎨 Design · UI/UX

One last plain summary + an honest "can't be undone". Re-auth (Face ID / fingerprint) and a deliberate confirm, never a stray tap. Easy back-out.

⚙️ Developer · API

Cancellation Confirm {bookingId, reference, cancelReason, refundRate} + our idempotency key. Reason → cancelReason 0–44. Then a processing state tolerant of the ~120s SLA / PENDING.

📈 Business

Deliberate friction prevents regret cancellations & fraud. Reason codes feed analytics and supply insight back to Agoda.

12 · PRODUCT NOTE  ①
Product note · the flow & the thinking

Built around one promise: no surprises.

The moment

Cancellation is the most emotional step in travel, plans fell through, money's at stake, a fight half-expected. Make it clear, fast and honest and a negative moment becomes a reason to trust us and rebook. Make it opaque and users call support, dispute with the bank, and leave one-star reviews.

The flow

Booking Details → Cancel → we call Agoda Preview and show the refund math, method, ETA and a live deadline countdown, the number is Agoda's, not ours. The screen adapts to Free / Partial / Non-refundable; the CTA states the outcome. Continue → optional reason → Confirm + re-auth → Agoda Confirm (with reference + idempotency key) → honest Processing (~120s, async-tolerant) → Confirmed with reference, refund & timeline, plus a receipt and status tracker.

Why these choices

  • Transparency over optimism, show the real refund up front; lose a few cancels, save far more in disputes.
  • Server-authoritative money, Agoda's number, surfaced, never recomputed on device.
  • Deliberate friction, re-auth + outcome-stating CTA protect users from regret & fraud, without feeling like a trap.
  • Designed for the unhappy path, the policy branches and error states are the product; the happy path is the simple part.

the refund shown is the refund paid.
